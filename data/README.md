Check each dataset's licence on its source page. The fire statistics file is a copy of open data published through THackle; its licence could not be read automatically (the portal needs JavaScript), so confirm the terms on the dataset page, and credit the Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation, before reusing it. The notebooks read the files from Google Drive paths such as `MyDrive/Super ai/Mini hack/Data.csv`; change them to wherever you put the files.
